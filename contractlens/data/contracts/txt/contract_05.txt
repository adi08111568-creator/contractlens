                  LETTER AGREEMENT - HOSPITAL SERVICES RATES AND TERMS
========================================================================================

Date: 17th day of August, 2025

This letter agreement sets out how Pinecrest Medical Plan (the Plan) will reimburse
Northgate Family Physicians (the Provider) for covered services given to the Plan's
members. It replaces the parties' earlier arrangement dated August 17, 2022, under which
clean claims were paid within 30 days and acute inpatient care was paid at $3,875 per
day. Those earlier terms no longer apply.

Rates. Starting on 17th day of August, 2025, the Plan will pay a flat per diem of $3,975
for each day of acute medical/surgical inpatient care, and one hundred fifty (150)
percent of the Medicare fee schedule for general outpatient services. Behavioral health
inpatient stays are paid at $4,275 per day, and emergency department visits at 165
percent of Medicare.

Payment and filing. The Plan will pay each clean claim within fifteen (15) days after it
receives the claim. The Provider must send claims no later than one hundred eighty (180)
days after the date of service. A denied claim may be appealed within 270 days.

Credentialing. The Plan will finish credentialing the Provider's clinicians within sixty
(60) days of getting a complete application.

Duration. This letter agreement begins on the 17th day of August, 2025, runs for four
years, and ends on the 16th day of August, 2029. At the end of that period it will roll
over from year to year unless one side gives notice of non-renewal 30 days ahead. Either
side may end it early, without cause, by giving sixty (60) days written notice.

Law. This letter agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Colorado.

Insurance and records. The Provider will carry professional liability cover of
$2,000,000 per occurrence and keep records for 7 years.
