                  LETTER AGREEMENT - HOSPITAL SERVICES RATES AND TERMS
========================================================================================

Date: 20th day of September, 2025

This letter agreement sets out how Evergreen Health Partners (the Plan) will reimburse
Larkspur Women's Health Center (the Provider) for covered services given to the Plan's
members. It replaces the parties' earlier arrangement dated September 20, 2022, under
which clean claims were paid within 60 days and acute inpatient care was paid at $3,600
per day. Those earlier terms no longer apply.

Rates. Starting on 20th day of September, 2025, the Plan will pay a flat per diem of
$3,750 for each day of acute medical/surgical inpatient care, and one hundred sixty
(160) percent of the Medicare fee schedule for general outpatient services. Behavioral
health inpatient stays are paid at $4,050 per day, and emergency department visits at
175 percent of Medicare.

Payment and filing. The Plan will pay each clean claim within forty-five (45) days after
it receives the claim. The Provider must send claims no later than three hundred
sixty-five (365) days after the date of service. A denied claim may be appealed within
210 days.

Credentialing. The Plan will finish credentialing the Provider's clinicians within
forty-five (45) days of getting a complete application.

Duration. This letter agreement begins on the 20th day of September, 2025, runs for four
years, and ends on the 19th day of September, 2029. It will not roll over; a new signed
agreement is needed to continue after that date. Either side may end it early, without
cause, by giving one hundred eighty (180) days written notice.

Law. This letter agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida.

Insurance and records. The Provider will carry professional liability cover of
$2,000,000 per occurrence and keep records for 10 years.
