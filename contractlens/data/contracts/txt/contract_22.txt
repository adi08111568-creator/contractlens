                        MASTER PARTICIPATING FACILITY AGREEMENT
========================================================================================

This Master Participating Facility Agreement is made on the 9th day of April, 2023
between Crestline Health Partners and Tamarack Oncology Partners.


1. DEFINITIONS

"Effective Date" means April 9, 2023. "Clean Claim" means a claim that can be processed
without obtaining additional information from the Provider. "Medicare Fee Schedule"
means the applicable CMS fee schedule in effect on the date of service. "Plan" means
Crestline Health Partners. "Facility" means Tamarack Oncology Partners.


2. TERM

2.1 The term of this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and ends on 08 Apr 2027,
unless sooner terminated.

2.2 Upon expiration of the term, this Agreement shall renew automatically for successive
twelve (12) month terms unless terminated in accordance with Section 2.3.

2.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause by giving 120 days written
notice.


3. COMPENSATION

3.1 Acute inpatient services shall be reimbursed at a per diem of $3,275.

3.2 General outpatient services shall be reimbursed at 90% of the Medicare Fee Schedule.


4. CLAIMS

4.1 The Plan shall pay each Clean Claim within 20 days of receipt. 4.2 The Facility
shall submit claims within 120 days of the date of service.


5. CREDENTIALING

5.1 The Plan shall complete credentialing within 45 days of a complete application.


6. GENERAL

6.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Georgia.

Confidentiality. Each party shall keep the financial terms of this Agreement
confidential and shall disclose them only as required by law.

Insurance. Provider shall maintain professional liability insurance of not less than
$2,000,000 per occurrence.

Records. Each party shall retain records relating to services for 7 years following the
date of service.

Disputes. The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through
informal negotiation for up to 30 days before pursuing other remedies.


AMENDMENT NO. 1

Effective April 9, 2024, the parties amend Sections 3.1 and 3.2 of the Agreement as set
out below. All other terms remain unchanged.

Section 3.1 is deleted and replaced with: "Acute inpatient services shall be reimbursed
at a per diem of $3,525."

Section 3.2 is deleted and replaced with: "General outpatient services shall be
reimbursed at 100% of the Medicare Fee Schedule."
