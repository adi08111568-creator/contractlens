                  LETTER AGREEMENT - HOSPITAL SERVICES RATES AND TERMS
========================================================================================

Date: 17th day of February, 2023

This letter agreement sets out how Crestline Health Partners (the Plan) will reimburse
St. Aldric Regional Medical Center (the Provider) for covered services given to the
Plan's members. It replaces the parties' earlier arrangement dated February 17, 2020,
under which clean claims were paid within 35 days and acute inpatient care was paid at
$3,775 per day. Those earlier terms no longer apply.

Rates. Starting on 17th day of February, 2023, the Plan will pay a flat per diem of
$3,925 for each day of acute medical/surgical inpatient care, and one hundred thirty
(130) percent of the Medicare fee schedule for general outpatient services. Behavioral
health inpatient stays are paid at $3,525 per day, and emergency department visits at
145 percent of Medicare.

Payment and filing. The Plan will pay each clean claim within twenty (20) days after it
receives the claim. The Provider must send claims no later than one hundred twenty (120)
days after the date of service. A denied claim may be appealed within 150 days.

Credentialing. The Plan will finish credentialing the Provider's clinicians within
ninety (90) days of getting a complete application.

Duration. This letter agreement begins on the 17th day of February, 2023, runs for three
years, and ends on the 16th day of February, 2026. At the end of that period it will
roll over from year to year unless one side gives notice of non-renewal 60 days ahead.
Either side may end it early, without cause, by giving one hundred twenty (120) days
written notice.

Law. This letter agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida.

Insurance and records. The Provider will carry professional liability cover of
$2,000,000 per occurrence and keep records for 10 years.
