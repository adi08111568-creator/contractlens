                  LETTER AGREEMENT - HOSPITAL SERVICES RATES AND TERMS
========================================================================================

Date: 4th day of December, 2022

This letter agreement sets out how Harborview Health Plan (the Plan) will reimburse
Marlow Children's Hospital (the Provider) for covered services given to the Plan's
members. It replaces the parties' earlier arrangement dated December 4, 2019, under
which clean claims were paid within 35 days and acute inpatient care was paid at $3,150
per day. Those earlier terms no longer apply.

Rates. Starting on 4th day of December, 2022, the Plan will pay a flat per diem of
$3,300 for each day of acute medical/surgical inpatient care, and one hundred ten (110)
percent of the Medicare fee schedule for general outpatient services. Behavioral health
inpatient stays are paid at $3,700 per day, and emergency department visits at 120
percent of Medicare.

Payment and filing. The Plan will pay each clean claim within twenty (20) days after it
receives the claim. The Provider must send claims no later than three hundred sixty-five
(365) days after the date of service. A denied claim may be appealed within 60 days.

Credentialing. The Plan will finish credentialing the Provider's clinicians within sixty
(60) days of getting a complete application.

Duration. This letter agreement begins on the 4th day of December, 2022, runs for five
years, and ends on the 3rd day of December, 2027. At the end of that period it will roll
over from year to year unless one side gives notice of non-renewal 30 days ahead. Either
side may end it early, without cause, by giving sixty (60) days written notice.

Law. This letter agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois.

Insurance and records. The Provider will carry professional liability cover of
$2,000,000 per occurrence and keep records for 10 years.
