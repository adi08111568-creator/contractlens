                  LETTER AGREEMENT - HOSPITAL SERVICES RATES AND TERMS
========================================================================================

Date: 12th day of January, 2025

This letter agreement sets out how Evergreen Health Partners (the Plan) will reimburse
St. Aldric Regional Medical Center (the Provider) for covered services given to the
Plan's members. It replaces the parties' earlier arrangement dated January 12, 2022,
under which clean claims were paid within 30 days and acute inpatient care was paid at
$3,775 per day. Those earlier terms no longer apply.

Rates. Starting on 12th day of January, 2025, the Plan will pay a flat per diem of
$3,875 for each day of acute medical/surgical inpatient care, and ninety-five (95)
percent of the Medicare fee schedule for general outpatient services. Behavioral health
inpatient stays are paid at $4,275 per day, and emergency department visits at 110
percent of Medicare.

Payment and filing. The Plan will pay each clean claim within fifteen (15) days after it
receives the claim. The Provider must send claims no later than one hundred twenty (120)
days after the date of service. A denied claim may be appealed within 150 days.

Credentialing. The Plan will finish credentialing the Provider's clinicians within
forty-five (45) days of getting a complete application.

Duration. This letter agreement begins on the 12th day of January, 2025, runs for three
years, and ends on the 11th day of January, 2028. It will not roll over; a new signed
agreement is needed to continue after that date. Either side may end it early, without
cause, by giving sixty (60) days written notice.

Law. This letter agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Michigan.

Insurance and records. The Provider will carry professional liability cover of
$2,000,000 per occurrence and keep records for 10 years.
