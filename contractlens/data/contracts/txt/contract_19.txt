                        MASTER PARTICIPATING FACILITY AGREEMENT
========================================================================================

This Master Participating Facility Agreement is made on the 26th day of July, 2022
between Summit Care Alliance and Marlow Children's Hospital.


1. DEFINITIONS

"Effective Date" means July 26, 2022. "Clean Claim" means a claim that can be processed
without obtaining additional information from the Provider. "Medicare Fee Schedule"
means the applicable CMS fee schedule in effect on the date of service. "Plan" means
Summit Care Alliance. "Facility" means Marlow Children's Hospital.


2. TERM

2.1 The term of this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and ends on 25 Jul 2026,
unless sooner terminated.

2.2 This Agreement does not renew automatically. Continuation after the expiration date
requires a signed written amendment.

2.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause by giving 180 days written
notice.


3. COMPENSATION

3.1 Acute inpatient services shall be reimbursed at a per diem of $3,050.

3.2 General outpatient services shall be reimbursed at 125% of the Medicare Fee
Schedule.


4. CLAIMS

4.1 The Plan shall pay each Clean Claim within 20 days of receipt. 4.2 The Facility
shall submit claims within 365 days of the date of service.


5. CREDENTIALING

5.1 The Plan shall complete credentialing within 45 days of a complete application.


6. GENERAL

6.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Pennsylvania.

Confidentiality. Each party shall keep the financial terms of this Agreement
confidential and shall disclose them only as required by law.

Insurance. Provider shall maintain professional liability insurance of not less than
$1,000,000 per occurrence.

Records. Each party shall retain records relating to services for 7 years following the
date of service.

Disputes. The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through
informal negotiation for up to 30 days before pursuing other remedies.


AMENDMENT NO. 1

Effective July 26, 2023, the parties amend Sections 3.1 and 3.2 of the Agreement as set
out below. All other terms remain unchanged.

Section 3.1 is deleted and replaced with: "Acute inpatient services shall be reimbursed
at a per diem of $3,200."

Section 3.2 is deleted and replaced with: "General outpatient services shall be
reimbursed at 130% of the Medicare Fee Schedule."
