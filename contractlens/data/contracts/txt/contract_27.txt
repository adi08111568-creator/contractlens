                        MASTER PARTICIPATING FACILITY AGREEMENT
========================================================================================

This Master Participating Facility Agreement is made on the 17th day of February, 2023
between Cobalt Mutual Health and Northgate Family Physicians.


1. DEFINITIONS

"Effective Date" means February 17, 2023. "Clean Claim" means a claim that can be
processed without obtaining additional information from the Provider. "Medicare Fee
Schedule" means the applicable CMS fee schedule in effect on the date of service. "Plan"
means Cobalt Mutual Health. "Facility" means Northgate Family Physicians.


2. TERM

2.1 The term of this Agreement begins on the Effective Date and ends on 16 Feb 2027,
unless sooner terminated.

2.2 This Agreement does not renew automatically. Continuation after the expiration date
requires a signed written amendment.

2.3 Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause by giving 60 days written
notice.


3. COMPENSATION

3.1 Acute inpatient services shall be reimbursed at a per diem of $2,875.

3.2 General outpatient services shall be reimbursed at 150% of the Medicare Fee
Schedule.


4. CLAIMS

4.1 The Plan shall pay each Clean Claim within 45 days of receipt. 4.2 The Facility
shall submit claims within 90 days of the date of service.


5. CREDENTIALING

5.1 The Plan shall complete credentialing within 90 days of a complete application.


6. GENERAL

6.1 This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Washington.

Confidentiality. Each party shall keep the financial terms of this Agreement
confidential and shall disclose them only as required by law.

Insurance. Provider shall maintain professional liability insurance of not less than
$2,000,000 per occurrence.

Records. Each party shall retain records relating to services for 10 years following the
date of service.

Disputes. The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through
informal negotiation for up to 45 days before pursuing other remedies.


AMENDMENT NO. 1

Effective February 17, 2024, the parties amend Sections 3.1 and 3.2 of the Agreement as
set out below. All other terms remain unchanged.

Section 3.1 is deleted and replaced with: "Acute inpatient services shall be reimbursed
at a per diem of $3,125."

Section 3.2 is deleted and replaced with: "General outpatient services shall be
reimbursed at 160% of the Medicare Fee Schedule."
