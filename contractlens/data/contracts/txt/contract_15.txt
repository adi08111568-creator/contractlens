EXHIBIT B - ADMINISTRATIVE TERMS

Item                     | Term
-------------------------+-----------------------------------

Confidentiality. Each party shall keep the financial terms of this Agreement
confidential and shall disclose them only as required by law.

Insurance. Provider shall maintain professional liability insurance of not less than
$2,000,000 per occurrence.

Records. Each party shall retain records relating to services for 10 years following the
date of service.

Disputes. The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through
informal negotiation for up to 30 days before pursuing other remedies.
