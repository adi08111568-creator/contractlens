                  LETTER AGREEMENT - HOSPITAL SERVICES RATES AND TERMS
========================================================================================

Date: 14th day of August, 2021

This letter agreement sets out how Evergreen Health Partners (the Plan) will reimburse
Ridgeway Rehabilitation Institute (the Provider) for covered services given to the
Plan's members. It replaces the parties' earlier arrangement dated August 14, 2018,
under which clean claims were paid within 35 days and acute inpatient care was paid at
$3,175 per day. Those earlier terms no longer apply.

Rates. Starting on 14th day of August, 2021, the Plan will pay a flat per diem of $3,375
for each day of acute medical/surgical inpatient care, and one hundred twenty-five (125)
percent of the Medicare fee schedule for general outpatient services. Behavioral health
inpatient stays are paid at $3,775 per day, and emergency department visits at 115
percent of Medicare.

Payment and filing. The Plan will pay each clean claim within twenty (20) days after it
receives the claim. The Provider must send claims no later than one hundred twenty (120)
days after the date of service. A denied claim may be appealed within 150 days.

Credentialing. The Plan will finish credentialing the Provider's clinicians within
thirty (30) days of getting a complete application.

Duration. This letter agreement begins on the 14th day of August, 2021, runs for three
years, and ends on the 13th day of August, 2024. It will not roll over; a new signed
agreement is needed to continue after that date. Either side may end it early, without
cause, by giving one hundred eighty (180) days written notice.

Law. This letter agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Ohio.

Insurance and records. The Provider will carry professional liability cover of
$2,000,000 per occurrence and keep records for 7 years.
