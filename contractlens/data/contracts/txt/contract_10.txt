                            PROVIDER PARTICIPATION AGREEMENT
========================================================================================

This Provider Participation Agreement (the "Agreement") is entered into as of August 7,
2024 (the "Effective Date") by and between Northbridge Assurance Company ("Plan") and
Northgate Family Physicians ("Provider").


ARTICLE I - TERM AND TERMINATION

1.1 Initial Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for an
initial term of 5 years, expiring on August 6, 2029.

1.2 Renewal. This Agreement shall not renew automatically. Any extension beyond the
initial term requires a written amendment signed by both parties.

1.3 Termination Without Cause. Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause
upon 60 days prior written notice to the other party.


ARTICLE II - COMPENSATION

2.1 Inpatient Services. Plan shall reimburse Provider for covered acute medical/surgical
inpatient services at a per diem rate of $2,325 per day. Behavioral health inpatient
services are reimbursed at $2,025 per day.

2.2 Outpatient Services. Plan shall reimburse Provider for covered general outpatient
services at 125% of the then-current Medicare fee schedule. Emergency department
services are reimbursed at 115% of the Medicare fee schedule.


ARTICLE III - CLAIMS

3.1 Payment of Clean Claims. Plan shall pay each clean claim within 15 days after
receipt.

3.2 Timely Filing. Provider shall submit claims within 365 days after the date of
service. Claim denials may be appealed within 270 days of the denial notice.


ARTICLE IV - CREDENTIALING

4.1 Plan shall complete credentialing of Provider's practitioners within 60 days after
receipt of a complete application.


ARTICLE V - GENERAL PROVISIONS

5.1 Governing Law. This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Florida.

Confidentiality. Each party shall keep the financial terms of this Agreement
confidential and shall disclose them only as required by law.

Insurance. Provider shall maintain professional liability insurance of not less than
$1,000,000 per occurrence.

Records. Each party shall retain records relating to services for 10 years following the
date of service.

Disputes. The parties shall attempt in good faith to resolve any dispute through
informal negotiation for up to 45 days before pursuing other remedies.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF, the parties have executed this Agreement as of the Effective Date.
